# Bristol Farms

Southern California premium grocery chain (founded 1982, Palos Verdes Peninsula), 13 stores plus a 14th opening in Hollywood in 2026. Owned by Good Food Holdings (which also owns Lazy Acres). Competes on heavy prepared-food operations (scratch kitchens, sushi, bakeries, cafés) at prices positioned below Erewhon — an external comparison point for Meridian's own prepared-foods/local-sourcing positioning.
