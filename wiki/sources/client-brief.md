# Client brief: Meridian Markets

Source: `raw/client-brief.md`

## Summary

An engagement brief from Dana Okafor (VP of Operations, Meridian Markets) to the LMU MSBA workshop team, dated August 2026. It introduces Meridian Markets, a 14-store specialty grocery chain, and asks for a dashboard analyzing sales performance by store and category to support a decision on the chain's next location (likely Pasadena) and to make better use of an underused loyalty program. It lists the data available, sets a non-negotiable rule that customer and employee data never go into AI tools, and gives a roughly eight-week timeline with a preliminary board check-in in three weeks.

## Key points

- Meridian Markets is a specialty grocery chain with fourteen stores across Los Angeles, Orange, and Ventura counties, roughly $78M in annual revenue, and about 620 employees; it grew from six to fourteen stores in five years by taking over leases from chains that pulled out of underserved neighborhoods (## About us).
- The team is asked to build a dashboard showing sales performance by store and category, to help leadership decide where to open the next location — Meridian believes Pasadena is the likely next site but wants data to confirm it (## What we need).
- Growth has been uneven across stores, decisions have so far been made "on instinct and a spreadsheet," and Meridian also wants to better use its loyalty program data (~40,000 members), which it hasn't really analyzed before (## Why now).
- Success means increased revenue, reduced operating costs, and improved customer experience, driven by better visibility into store and category performance (## What success looks like).
- Available data: ~3 years of POS transactions, loyalty program membership and purchase history (~40,000 members), labor scheduling and hours, and store attributes (square footage, opening date, lease terms); Meridian migrated to a new POS system last spring. IT (contact: Marcus) can provide a data extract once the NDA is signed (## Data we have).
- An NDA will follow the brief and covers everything in it. Customer records and employee data — including loyalty program data, labor schedules, and any excerpts of them — must never go into ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or any other AI tool; this is non-negotiable per Meridian's counsel. Sales totals by store and week, and store attributes, are explicitly fine to use with those tools (## Terms of engagement).
- Timeline is roughly eight weeks, with a preliminary version wanted for a board meeting in three weeks (## Timeline).
- Dana Okafor is the point of contact (email preferred); she travels Tuesdays and Wednesdays and can be slow to reply. Her assistant can schedule time but cannot answer analytics questions (## Reaching me).
