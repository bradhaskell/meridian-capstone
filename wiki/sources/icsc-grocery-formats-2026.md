# New Grocery Formats, Store Growth and Investment Activity Signal a Sector in Motion

Source: `raw/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md`

## Summary

An ICSC/*Commerce + Communities Today* industry piece (published April 24, 2026) on how the ~$912.4B U.S. grocery market is shifting: specialty and fresh-format grocers are gaining share from traditional chains, big-box players are reinvesting in remodels and new stores, grocery-anchored real estate investment is surging, and retailers are experimenting with store formats and footprints. This is external market context, not Meridian-specific data — useful backdrop for Meridian's own competitive positioning (prepared foods, local sourcing, smaller footprint) and expansion decisions.

## Key points

- U.S. grocery market estimated at $912.4B (IBISWorld); value-focused and fresh-format operators are gaining influence over traditional grocery anchors (## Grocery-anchor performance is diverging)
- Trader Joe's grew 3% YoY, outperforming the sector by 6 points, with double-digit growth among Gen Z shoppers; Publix and Safeway declined across all income groups; Aldi/Food 4 Less growth leveled off since mid-2025 (## Specialty grocers gain share)
- Target is differentiating via specialty/wellness/owned brands rather than becoming a conventional full-line grocer; food & beverage produced $24B+ in FY2025 net sales (## Target emphasizes differentiation and experience)
- Walmart is remodeling 650+ stores and adding ~20 new stores through 2026-27; Costco plans ~28 net new stores and operates ~640 U.S. warehouses (## Walmart and Costco expand and reinvest in stores)
- Aldi is piloting a modular, flexible store format (Aventura, FL) that adapts to different real-estate sizes, and plans 150 U.S. store openings in 2026 for its 50th U.S. anniversary (## Aldi pilots a flexible layout)
- U.S. grocery-anchored center transactions totaled ~$11B in 2025, up 42% from 2024; major buyers include Brixton Capital, Blackstone Real Estate, and Nuveen (## Grocery-anchored investment remains substantial)
- Grocery and convenience stores are gaining visits at the expense of restaurants, per Tillster's 2026 Phygital Index Report (## Grocery and convenience stores compete with restaurants)
- Gelson's (Southern California, 27 stores) is piloting small-format "premium convenience stores" (3,000-5,400 sq ft) paired with EV charging hubs (## Gelson's combines small-format grocery with EV charging)
