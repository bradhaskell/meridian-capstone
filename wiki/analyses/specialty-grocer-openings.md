# Where are specialty grocers opening?

## Question

What do our sources say about where specialty grocers are opening?

## Answer

- **Whole Foods** (specialty/premium) is anchoring a new Regency Centers development, "The Marketplace," in **Redlands, California** — 36,000 sq ft within a 71,400 sq ft center. ([sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md))
- **Bristol Farms** (premium specialty chain) is opening its **14th store in Hollywood, Los Angeles**, in 2026. ([sources/hoodline-bristol-farms-erewhon-2026.md](../sources/hoodline-bristol-farms-erewhon-2026.md))
- **Meridian Markets** itself (the client, positioned as specialty via prepared foods/local sourcing) is weighing **Pasadena, CA** as its next location. ([sources/client-brief.md](../sources/client-brief.md))
- **Aldi** is piloting a flexible modular format starting in **Aventura, Florida**. Note: the ICSC source categorizes Aldi as a *discount* grocer, not specialty, so this is a borderline fit for the question. ([sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md](../sources/icsc-grocery-formats-2026.md))

**Own conclusion (not stated by any source):** three of these four locations — Redlands, Hollywood, and Pasadena — are all in Southern California/greater LA. None of the ingested sources draw this connection themselves; it's an observation from comparing them side by side.
