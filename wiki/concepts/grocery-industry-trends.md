# Grocery industry trends (2026)

External market context for Meridian Markets' competitive positioning and expansion decisions: specialty/fresh-format grocers gaining share from traditional chains, big-box reinvestment in remodels and new stores, surging grocery-anchored real estate investment, and store-format experimentation (smaller footprints, EV charging, modular layouts). Not Meridian-specific data.
