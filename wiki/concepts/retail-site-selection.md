# Retail site selection

A structured framework for evaluating a new store location: map the existing network, measure competitive/co-tenant effects, match candidate sites to the actual customer profile (not just population totals), check physical/financial/legal feasibility, analyze current-store performance, size the footprint correctly, weight criteria by retail vertical (grocery specifically: parking, circulation, basket-oriented access, competitive saturation), and validate the forecast after opening. Directly applicable to Meridian's Pasadena decision.
